# Science Defender — Art & Animation Audit (2026-10-24)

Method: contact sheets of every enemy, boss and hero strip (`docs/evidence/art-audit-2026-10-24/`),
per-frame measurement of centre drift and size variance (Python/PIL), a simulation of enemy facing
along all 20 routes, and a read of the render code. Facing claims were checked against
the code path that draws each sprite.

## How facing works today

All production art is meant to be **right-facing three-quarter view** (`js/game.js:44`). The renderer
mirrors a sprite when it moves left:
- enemies: `facingX` from the route tangent (`js/enemies.js:224`) → `ctx.scale(facingX…)` (`js/enemies.js:271`)
- heroes: `faceLeft` from movement/target (`js/heroes.js:277`, `:327`) → mirror (`js/heroes.js:509`)
- barracks troops / Darwin's tortoise (`SummonedMinion.draw`, `js/heroes.js:97`): **never mirrored**

Route sampling is clean: no rapid flip-flopping on any level, and 11 of 20 levels never flip at all
(they run left → right).

## Findings (most visible first)

1. **Three enemies are drawn facing LEFT, so they walk backwards.** `flatearth`, `phlogiston` and
   `golem` (`assets/enemies/candidates/*_walk_v2.png`) face left; the renderer assumes right. On every
   rightward stretch (most of most levels) they moonwalk, and they turn "forward" only when the road
   heads left. Flat Earth and Phlogiston are the Level 1 enemies, so this is the first impression.
   (`facing_zoom.png`)
2. **Troops and the tortoise never turn.** They always face right; enemies usually arrive from the
   left, so blockers stand with their backs to what they're fighting. They also loop the **walk** cycle
   while standing still (marching in place) — there is no idle/attack state for troops.
3. **Enemies have no attack or blocked state.** A blocked enemy keeps playing its walk cycle in place
   *(inferred from code: only a walk strip exists per enemy)*. Realm Defense swaps to an attack loop.
4. **Frames change size within one cycle (pulsing).** Height/width range across a walk cycle:
   aether 43% h, vitalism 38% w, homunculus_core 38% h, boss_entropy 41% w, golem_molten 28% h,
   boss_phlog 28% w, maggot 26% h, caloric 24% h. Heroes are tight (1–3% drift) except Newton walk
   (17% h). `golem_molten` and `homunculus_core` frames read as different poses/creatures rather than
   a cycle; `aether`/`boss_aether` re-shape every frame.
5. **Neighbour-row spill in hero strips.** Einstein and Newton attack/walk cells show fragments of
   the next row's effects (cyan rings / purple arcs) under the feet; a stray speck sits left of
   several Field Team frames; Flat Earth's crossbow is clipped at the right cell edge. (`hero_states.png`, `heroes_walk.png`)
6. **Three art styles on screen at once.** Characters/enemies are 64 px painterly *pixel art*, towers
   are 128 px illustrated sprites, backgrounds are 1800×1100 smooth painted cartoon, UI is clean vector.
   Pixel art is upscaled with `imageSmoothingEnabled = true` (`js/game.js:1515`), so it renders soft and
   mushy at 2–3× and clashes with the crisp UI. Realm Defense uses one smooth cartoon style throughout.
7. **Readability at phone scale.** Enemies render ~15 px tall in landscape on an iPhone; several sheets
   are low-contrast dark-on-dark (phlogiston, creationist, boss_entropy) against dark biomes.
8. Minor: only the current level's enemy art is preloaded, so code paths that spawn an off-roster type
   fall back to the vector placeholder box (seen in a debug spawn; not reachable in normal play).

## Fix plan

**A. Code-only (no new art, ~1 hour)**
1. Add a `nativeFacing: -1` flag to `SHEET_META` for `enemy_flatearth_walk`, `enemy_phlogiston_walk`,
   `enemy_golem_walk`, and multiply it into `facingX` at draw time. Add a test that every enemy/hero
   sheet declares or defaults its facing. (Alternative: mirror those three PNGs once on disk.)
2. Give `SummonedMinion` a `faceLeft` set from its nearest blocked enemy (same rule as heroes), and
   hold frame 0 (or a slow breathing bob) when not moving instead of looping walk.
3. Choose one scaling rule per style: if keeping pixel art, draw characters with smoothing **off** at
   integer scales; keep smoothing on for painted backgrounds.

**B. Asset cleanup (scriptable with the existing `scripts/pack-*.py` tools)**
4. Re-pack strips with a fixed scale per character (normalise by the median frame, not per frame) to
   remove pulsing; clear spill by masking each cell to its largest connected component + effect layer.
5. Re-cut the Flat Earth sheet with padding so the crossbow isn't clipped.

**C. Art regeneration (where PixelLab helps)**
6. Regenerate the 6 worst cycles (`golem_molten`, `homunculus_core`, `aether`, `boss_aether`,
   `vitalism`, `boss_entropy`) as proper walk loops facing right, same character per frame.
7. Add a 4–6 frame **attack** strip for each regular enemy and an **idle/attack** pair for troops.
8. Decide the house style. Recommendation: commit to pixel art for actors (PixelLab's strength,
   consistent rotations and animation from one reference) *and* add a pixel/posterised treatment to
   the outlines of towers so they sit with the actors; keep painted backgrounds but darken/desaturate
   them slightly behind the road so 64 px actors pop. Generating directional variants (side + up/down
   three-quarter) would also stop vertical road sections looking like side-scrolling.
9. Raise actor contrast: 1–2 px dark outline + soft light rim on every enemy sheet.
